Instructions

What is a Free Body Diagram?

A free body diagram (FBD) is a simplified drawing in physics and engineering that isolates a single object and shows all the external forces, moments, and reactions acting on it. It removes the surrounding environment and replaces physical connections or supports with arrows that represent those interactions.

Available Forces

  • Applied Force: A push or pull exerted on an object by a person or another object.
  • Gravitational Force: The attractive force that pulls any two objects with mass toward each other.
  • Normal Force: The support force exerted by a surface on an object.
  • Frictional Force: A force that opposes motion between surfaces in contact.

Using the Diagram Maker

  1. Select the forces you want to include in your diagram. Gravity is automatically included.
  2. Enter the magnitude and angle for each selected force.
  3. Choose either mass or acceleration and enter its value.
  4. Select "Show Net Force" to display the resultant force.
  5. Click "Reset" to clear the inputs and start a new diagram.
